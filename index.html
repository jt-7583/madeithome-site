How it works

1

Set a curfew

Pick tonight's be-home-by time for each kid. Add a private grace period if you like — they only see the deadline.

2

They check in at home

The Check In button only appears on your approved home Wi-Fi, confirmed with Face ID. "Almost home" doesn't count.

3

Miss it? Your phone rings

A real call-style alert that breaks through silent mode and keeps ringing — with their last known location.

4

An "I need help" button

Kids can send an instant SOS with their location, any time they feel unsafe.

What it doesn't do

No live map. No following your kid around all day. No selling data, no ads. Check in on time and nobody ever sees where they've been — the app only ever knows one thing: home, or not yet.
